# Past Material

Previous years' exams, quizzes and homework for CMI BSc Math & Physics courses, collected on 2 Oct 2026.

Folders go `Sem N / Course / <year> - <instructor> (<source>)`. Instructors change from year to year, so check whose papers you're looking at.

- **Arjun Maneesh Agarwal** (2024 Fall offerings of the Sem 1 courses): https://thearjunagarwal.github.io/courses.html
- **fourthground** (Tejas Sah and Soham Saha; 2025 Fall, 2026 Spring and the in-progress 2026 Fall): https://fourthground.codeberg.page/prev_material/index.html

## Notes from Arjun's page (2024)
- **Algebra 1 (Karnataki):** no prescribed textbook, and he recommends Treil's *Linear Algebra Done Wrong*. The problems are hard, but grade cutoffs were lenient. The homeworks were quiz prep and weren't submitted.
- **Analysis 1 (Kulkarni):** Baby Rudin. He used Abbott's *Understanding Analysis* instead (solutions are included). The exams ran very long, so carry snacks. The other homeworks were Rudin exercises and aren't included.
- **Haskell (Suresh):** taught from Prof. Suresh's slides, with Hutton as a backup. The course was to be overhauled from 2025 onwards around a new textbook by Hota, Sharma and Agarwal.
- **CM 1 (Virmani):** Morin. Use Morin's relativity book for the relativity part, Kevin Zhou's handouts (https://knzhou.github.io/) for harder problems, and these YouTube videos for Lagrangians: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUAfsXEeYFc, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8UtnDaGHpq0, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dPxhTiiq-1A
- **English (Mahadevan):** the literature changes every year. The linguistics and speaking parts aren't in the materials.

There is some history behind this book. My friend gifted to me on my (fake) birthday and this is the book that got me into reading again, so I have fond, rose tinted memories of it. Now I can perhaps see that the prose was pretentious at times and that Zusak's decisions to inflict all possible forms of suffering were not very great, but there is a lot of time to go before I read this again, and till then, it does deserve its flowers for me.
